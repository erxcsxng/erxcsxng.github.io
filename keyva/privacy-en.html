Keyva Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Acid Monk ("we") makes and runs Keyva, a keycap keyboard app ("the app"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it.

At a glance
  • What you type with the keyboard is never stored or sent off your phone.
  • Signing in is optional. If you don't sign in, we receive no personal information.
  • If you sign in, your themes are backed up to your account so you can use them on a new phone.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details.
  • No ads, no analytics, and we never sell data.

1. Keyboard input

The keyboard processes key presses on your phone and inserts them into the text field. Typed text, passwords and copied content are never stored or transmitted. The "Paste" chip above the keyboard reads your phone's clipboard only to show it on screen; it is not sent anywhere.

3. Phone permissions

  • Microphone (optional): used only while you record in "Make your own sound". Recordings are stored on your phone; if you're signed in, only the trimmed sound (up to 1 second) is backed up.
  • Notifications (optional): used for a single reminder two days before your 7-day free trial ends.
  • Photos and audio files: you pick files in your phone's own picker, so the app never asks for access to your whole photo library.

If you don't sign in, no data is transferred. Every feature except backup works without signing in.

5. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: Settings › Account › Delete account. Your backup and account are deleted immediately and can't be restored.
  • By email: if you no longer have the app, email monkmindlab@gmail.com with the Google email you signed in with. After confirming it's you, we delete everything without delay and within 30 days at the latest.
  • Signing out does not delete your backup.
  • To stop a subscription, cancel it separately in Google Play › Subscriptions.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your information, or ask us to stop processing it, at any time — directly in the app's settings or by emailing us below.

7. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS), and access rules let only you read and write your own backup. We do not look at backup contents unless required to.

8. Children

Sign-in and backup are not intended for children under 14 (or the minimum age in your country). If we learn that such a child's information was collected without guardian consent, we delete it promptly.
