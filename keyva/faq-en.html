Keyva FAQ

Updated November 25, 2026

Turning on the keyboard

How do I turn on Keyva?

In the app, go to Settings › Help › How to turn on the keyboard and follow the steps. Turn on Keyva in your phone's keyboard list, then pick Keyva in the keyboard picker. To find the list yourself, search for "keyboard" in your phone's Settings.

A warning says the keyboard "may be able to collect all the text you type"

Android shows the same notice for every keyboard app. Keyva never stores what you type and never sends it off your phone. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I switch back to another keyboard?

Long-press Keyva's space bar to open the keyboard picker, then choose the keyboard you want.

Sound and vibration

I can't hear key sounds

  • If the app's home screen says the volume is at 0, tap it to turn the volume up.
  • Keyva follows media volume by default. You can switch to system volume in Settings › Sound › Key sounds follow.
  • To hear sounds while your phone is on silent or vibrate, turn on "Play sounds in silent mode" in Settings › Sound.
  • Also check that you haven't picked "No sound" in Customize › Sound.

Can I change the vibration strength?

Turn on "Vibrate on key press" in Settings › Sound, and you can choose from 5 strength levels.

Layouts and customizing

I want to type Korean with Cheonjiin or Naratgeul

On the home screen, tap Customize › Typing › Korean layout to choose Dubeolsik, Danmoeum, Cheonjiin, Cheonjiin Plus or Naratgeul. Cheonjiin-style layouts and Naratgeul also come in a centered version.

How do favorite characters work?

Long-press a symbol on the symbols screen to add it to the row above the keyboard. Long-press it in that row to remove it. Favorite characters are a Pro feature.

Payments and Pro

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan comes with a 7-day free trial, once per account. If you allow notifications, we'll remind you two days before it ends. Cancel in Google Play at least 24 hours before it ends and you won't be charged.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the Google Play app, go to Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. You can also tap Settings › Keyva Pro › Manage subscription in the app. After you cancel, you keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

Is Lifetime really a one-time payment?

Yes. Pay once and keep Pro, with no renewals and no extra charges.

I got a new phone and lost Pro

Sign in to the Play Store with the Google account you paid with, then tap Settings › Keyva Pro › Restore purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Google Play handles payments, so refunds are requested through Google Play. Pick the Keyva purchase in your Google Play order history to request one. If you run into trouble, email us.

Account and backup

Do I need to sign in?

No, signing in is optional. If you sign in, your themes and keyboard settings are backed up to your account, so they come back when you sign in on a new phone.

How do I delete my account and data?

Tap Settings › Account › Delete account and it's deleted right away. See how to delete your account and data for more.
